      Pipeline

      How it's made. Four steps behind every piece.

      Every frame is built with intent, from clean topology and expressive rigging to final composite, light and grade.

      1. Design & Model

        Block out 3D characters and environments in Blender, or design 2D assets and branching narrative worlds.

      2. Rig

        Build armatures, controls and clean weight painting in Blender, or set up 2D character puppets.

      3. Animate

        Key body mechanics, seamless locomotion cycles and expressive character performance frame by frame.

      4. Composite & Finish

        Light and render 3D shots, composite in After Effects, and finish the cut and sound in Premiere Pro.
